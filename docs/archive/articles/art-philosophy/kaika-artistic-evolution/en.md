## Overview

On January 14, 2024, at Yoyogi 1st Gymnasium during her 4th ONE-MAN LIVE "Kaika", KAF unveiled her parallel singer-songwriter persona **KAIKA**, performing with an acoustic guitar.

## Breaking the Virtual Boundary

Rather than choosing between staying behind an avatar or abandoning lore through graduation, the dual-existence paradigm allows KAF to embody grand mythic narratives while KAIKA channels intimate, personal songwriting, pioneering a sustainable path for digital performers.
