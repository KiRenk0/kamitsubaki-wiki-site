## Overview and Background

THINKR inc. (founded in 2016) operates as a "Dramatic Design Firm" led by CEO Kenjiro Hariya and Executive Producer PIEDPIPER. In summer 2024, THINKR executed a historic Management Buyout (MBO) from Avex Group and raised approximately 5 billion JPY led by KDDI Open Innovation Fund 3 to establish complete creative and corporate independence.

## Key Figures and Philosophy

- **PIEDPIPER**: Founder and Chief Creative Officer of KAMITSUBAKI STUDIO who discovered KAF and pioneered the virtual singer movement prioritizing genuine musicianship over standard livestreaming.
- **Kenjiro Hariya**: Veteran digital culture producer who safeguarded creative autonomy and spearheaded the MBO restructuring.

## Autonomous Media Ecosystem

With full independence, THINKR unified its core labels and expanded into transmedia operations, including the PNDR music distribution platform and the Kamitsubaki City multimedia franchise.
