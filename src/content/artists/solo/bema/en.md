## Overview

BEMA (琶舞) is a virtual singer who debuted on April 30, 2025 (entry to be expanded). With the September 5, 2026 renewal of [KAMITSUBAKI STUDIO](/en/projects/labels/kamitsubaki-studio), the in-studio labels were consolidated into PHENOMENON RECORD, which now represents her (the [SINSEKAI RECORD](/en/projects/archive/sinsekai-studio) name was retired).
