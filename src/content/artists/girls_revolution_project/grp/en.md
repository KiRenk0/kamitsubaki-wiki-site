## Overview

GIRLS REVOLUTION PROJECT (少女革命計画) is a new music × story label from KAMITSUBAKI STUDIO. Centered on the Xtuber unit SINSEIKI, which crosses the boundary between virtual and real, and the virtual singer unit TSUMITOBATSU, multiple artists and composers take part in a story linked to music.

The catchphrase is “A SECRET PROTOCOL THAT HELPS YOU EVOLVE. EVOLVE, ELECTRONIC GIRLS!”

## Staff

- Producer: PIEDPIPER
- Original Story: KAMITSUBAKI STUDIO
- Art Direction: PHASE STUDIO
- Character Design: PALOW.

## Units

- **SINSEIKI (心世紀)**: an Xtuber unit formed by ORIHIME, KAKYOIN, and GARASUMIYA.
- **TSUMITOBATSU (罪十罰)**: a virtual singer unit formed by MIKOTO, YUNAGI, and HINAGESHI.

## Selected Works

- Dual-unit songs: “改変”, “主人行路”, “鈍色幻灯”, “現世回帰”, “クロマティック feat. ヰ世界情緒”
- Albums: “改変 -心-” (SINSEIKI), “改変 -罪-” (TSUMITOBATSU)
