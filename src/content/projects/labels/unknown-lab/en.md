## Introduction

*UNKNOWN LAB* is a research & development department newly established by THINKR in the studio reorganization announced on September 5, 2026. It is operated around PIEDPIPER, the general producer of *[KAMITSUBAKI STUDIO](/en/projects/labels/kamitsubaki-studio)*, and serves as an experimental ground for nurturing new businesses and projects.

In THINKR's new studio architecture, UNKNOWN LAB forms the "RESEARCH & DEVELOPMENT" division on its own, alongside "MUSIC & EXPERIENCE" (KAMITSUBAKI STUDIO / [KYOKAI STUDIO](/en/projects/labels/kyokai-studio) / [ALLT STUDIO](/en/projects/labels/allt-studio)) and "CREATIVE & ART" (QA STUDIO / PHASE STUDIO). As of September 2026, UNKNOWN LAB has no dedicated website of its own, and the details of its projects have not been made public.

## Role and Positioning

- **An experimental ground for new businesses**: As THINKR's in-house R&D department, it handles the incubation, prototyping, and validation of new businesses and projects.
- **Led by PIEDPIPER**: It is personally led by PIEDPIPER, the general producer of KAMITSUBAKI STUDIO, marking an extension of the founding producer's focus toward the exploration of new domains.
