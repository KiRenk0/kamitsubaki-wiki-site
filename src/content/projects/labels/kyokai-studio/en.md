## Introduction

*KYOKAI STUDIO* is a music studio under THINKR, officially established on September 5, 2026 in the form of a spin-off from *[KAMITSUBAKI STUDIO](/en/projects/labels/kamitsubaki-studio)* as part of THINKR's studio reorganization. The studio works with "artists who keep exploring their own boundaries" and pursues **solid and free music**.

Under THINKR's new architecture, KAMITSUBAKI STUDIO will focus on the virtual domain, while the roster of real-life artists and creators who were active on the internet is carried over mainly by KYOKAI STUDIO. Within THINKR's "MUSIC & EXPERIENCE" division, KYOKAI STUDIO sits alongside KAMITSUBAKI STUDIO and [ALLT STUDIO](/en/projects/labels/allt-studio).

The official website (kyokaistudio.jp) went live ahead of the announcement, in late August 2026, publishing the studio's artist / creator roster.

## History

- Late August 2026: The official website (kyokaistudio.jp) goes live, revealing the studio's artist / creator roster and works.
- September 5, 2026: THINKR announces its studio reorganization, formally establishing KYOKAI STUDIO as a spin-off from KAMITSUBAKI STUDIO. The same day, the website opens "KYOKAI STUDIO AUDITION" to recruit next-generation artists.
- September 2026: Multiple artists and creators formerly under the KAMITSUBAKI STUDIO system (ANARCHIC RECORD, SINSEKAI RECORD, CREATOR FARM, etc.) are confirmed to have transferred.
