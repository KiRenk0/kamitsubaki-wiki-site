## Introduction

*ALLT STUDIO* is a music label under THINKR, operating under the names "ALLT STUDIO" and "ALLT RECORD". Its concept, per the official website, is "a Japan-born music creator label that manages artists active mainly in the net scene". The official website is alltstudio.jp.

In THINKR's new studio architecture announced on September 5, 2026, ALLT STUDIO belongs to the "MUSIC & EXPERIENCE" division alongside [KAMITSUBAKI STUDIO](/en/projects/labels/kamitsubaki-studio) and [KYOKAI STUDIO](/en/projects/labels/kyokai-studio). Artists from the former SINSEKAI RECORD ([SINSEKAI RECORD](/en/projects/archive/sinsekai-studio)) system — Aru, Shido, Mizuno Atsu, [Sooda](/en/artists/creators/sooda), and the unit Amayadori — are all listed on the ALLT STUDIO website's artist roster.
