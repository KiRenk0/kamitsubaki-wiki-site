## Overview

KAF’s 3rd ONE-MAN LIVE, Fukakai San Kyou, took place at Nippon Budokan on 24 August 2022. The official Blu-ray contains the main programme, a separately labelled bonus section, making-of material and documentaries.

## Setlist coverage

This page records 30 musical chapters from the official Blu-ray main programme, retaining BD chapter numbers and named guests. MC, interludes and the separately labelled bonus section are excluded. The edited recording does not establish the complete original concert sequence, so coverage remains partial. Entries whose work version cannot be confirmed retain titles without a work link.
