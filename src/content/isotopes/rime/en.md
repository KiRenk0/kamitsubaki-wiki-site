## Overview

**RIME** is a Musical Isotope based on virtual singer [RIM’s voice](/en/database/artists/solo/rim/). The CeVIO AI singing product was released on 25 October 2022; the VOICEPEAK TALK EXTENSION text-to-speech product followed on 26 February 2024.

## Products and design references

CeVIO AI serves singing production and VOICEPEAK serves text-to-speech; these are distinct products. PALOW. published RIME reference sheets on the singing product’s release day. PHASE STUDIO links the same official account. Publication of these sheets supports the recorded design attribution, but direct confirmation of an explicit design credit remains outstanding.

Consult the [official singing product page](https://musical-isotope.kamitsubaki.jp/product/rime/) and [official text-to-speech product page](https://musical-isotope.kamitsubaki.jp/product/rimevoicepeak/) for their purposes, requirements and use conditions.
