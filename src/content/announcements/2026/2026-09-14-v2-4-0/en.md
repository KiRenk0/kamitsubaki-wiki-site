**V2.4.0 brings a more consistent experience to reading, exploring and contributing.**

## Shared navigation and motion

The contribution hub, My space and LABs share headers, buttons and navigation components. Selection indicators and panels transition together; navigation becomes compact while scrolling. Keyboard navigation and reduced-motion preferences remain supported.

## Reading with a sense of place

Artist, song and album pages use their existing artwork as a blurred background. A reading mask keeps text clear, while the shared reader brings together the contents list, article and information cards. Pages without artwork retain a plain background.

Spoilers are blurred by default and preview on hover. The first click reveals the content; a concealed link requires a second click to open.

## Clearer contribution paths

The [contribution hub](/en/contribute/) gathers quick start, in-site editing, GitHub submissions, image and file handling, syntax, formatting and licensing in one place.

The [editor](/en/contribute/editor/) uses site typography with clearer toolbar, entry properties and entry selection layouts. File guides explain GitHub uploads, image paths and classification without requiring contributors to compress originals.

## Smaller details, smoother use

[My space](/en/account/) follows the shared layout. The login dialog includes GitHub and Google icons, fixes background scrolling and restores the previous page position when closed.

The custom cursor is consistent across the site, including readers, the editor and dialogs. Special collaborator profiles expand without stretching their text. Click anywhere or press Esc to skip the opening animation.

Submissions still require maintainer review. Report issues with a page link and reproduction steps through the [contact entry](/en/#social-contact).
