Hello, observers.

This update makes it easier to organize records and gives community projects a place where their history can stay accessible.

## 01 · Observer Projects

The [community calendar](/en/community/activities/) brings together meetups, support campaigns, creative projects, and online events. Switch between a calendar and an agenda, filter by location or participation mode, and explore event details and earlier editions. Calendar export and subscriptions are available too.

Organizers and observers can [apply for promotion or contribute information](/en/community/activities/apply/). Drafts, review feedback, and revisions are retained. Pending changes keep the current public version intact. Online events do not require a venue.

## 02 · A new editing workbench

[Entry editing](/en/contribute/editor/) and [article submissions](/en/articles/submit/) now use a new workbench with clearer controls for writing, properties, outlines, tables, images, and special content.

Images follow the existing staging and review process, with captions, creators, and sources. Drafts, exports, recovery, submission progress, and review feedback remain available. If versions conflict, your current writing is kept so you can compare the changes.

Entries and articles retain their respective review processes. Saving or uploading is a separate step from publication.

## 03 · Consistent controls and ways to support

Calendar controls, forms, floating buttons, and management screens have been refined. The support and calendar buttons now share interaction feedback.

The [support page](/en/support/) adds Alipay and WeChat alongside Afdian. You choose the method and amount, and can inspect the costs and funding records. Automatic reminders can be disabled. Reading remains free.

## Coming next · Account screens and core experience

The next phase will prioritize account screens and consolidate repeated implementations through mature open-source components. We will introduce improvements after validation and announce timing separately.

Thank you to everyone who adds sources, corrects records, contributes projects, and supports the site.
