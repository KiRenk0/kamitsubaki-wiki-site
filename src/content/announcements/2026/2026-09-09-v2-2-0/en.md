Hello, observers.

**V2.2.0 introduces My space.**

Accounts previously lived mainly in the AI observation terminal. Now you can use yours across the Wiki, save works and articles you want to revisit, and find them on another device.

**Reading the Wiki still requires no login.** Accounts help you keep your profile, bookmarks, and lists.

## One account for the Wiki and AI terminal

Sign in with **GitHub or Google**. If you already use the AI terminal, continue with your original sign-in method.

You can verify and link another sign-in method in My space. Separate accounts are not automatically merged by email. To unlink a method, first verify with the one you want to keep. At least one method must remain; sessions created through the removed method are also signed out.

## Your name and avatar

Your first sign-in uses the default name and avatar provided by GitHub or Google. You can then edit your name, choose a preset avatar, or upload an image.

Uploads accept JPG, PNG, and WebP up to 5 MB and are cropped to a centered square. After you save a custom profile, signing in again will not replace it with the provider’s defaults.

## Bookmarks and lists across devices

Signed-in bookmarks and lists sync to your account and are **private by default**. Guest bookmarks stay in the browser; preview them before choosing whether to merge them.

Local changes remain available if the connection fails. Retry when connected, or sync manually. If two devices change the same content, you can export a backup and choose the cloud or local version. Choosing the local version replaces cloud bookmarks and lists, so review the confirmation carefully.

Profile editing also protects unsaved drafts: leaving the page prompts you, updates from another device retain your draft, and switching accounts clears the previous account’s draft.

## Manage your sessions and data

View sign-in sessions, revoke one session, or sign out all other devices. Account details and bookmarks have one export; AI conversations and memories have a separate export.

You can also request account deletion. An administrator reviews and processes the request; submission does not delete the account immediately, and you can cancel before processing begins.
