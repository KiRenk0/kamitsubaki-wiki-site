Hello, observers.

**The v2.1.0 update notes are here.**

I have been tidying up the Wiki again: the reader, article editing, LABs, and the announcement you are reading right now all got some attention.

Here is what changed.

## 📖 A fresh pass over the reader

Some pages gave their headers half the screen before you could get very far into the article. Those headers are smaller now. Text size, line spacing, and paragraph spacing have been adjusted too, along with the contents, favorites, and editing controls.

I also tidied up the artist categories for songs and albums. Try an article you know well. If something still feels cramped or leaves a ridiculous amount of empty space, send over the page link.

## ✍️ Want to add to an article?

The **visual editor** is ready to try!

Open an existing article and keep editing, or start a new one. You can work with headings, paragraphs, links, and images in the editor, and switch to source whenever you want.

If you have been meaning to add something about an artist you love, give it a go. A date, a source, a couple of lines of translation: all welcome. There are still plenty of gaps in the Wiki for us to fill together.

[Edit an article](/en/contribute/edit/) · [Writing and sources guide](/en/contribute/format/) · [Syntax cheat sheet](/en/contribute/syntax/)

## 🧭 A new sign on the BETA door: LABs

Look for the timeline, relationship graph, and other ways to explore under **LABs**.

You can switch sections within the page, and each one has a link to bookmark. Memory Corridor and the AI Observation Terminal are still around too; head over from the homepage whenever you feel like it.

[Have a look around LABs](/en/labs/)

## 📋 Proper formatting for announcements, at last

Yes, this very window.

Announcements now use the same parser as articles, so **bold text, links, lists, and expandable notes** can go straight in. Long notices scroll inside the window, with the close button staying where it is.

That gives me a little more room to break things up when there is a lot to share.

## 🤝 And the special contributors list

The homepage list has been tidied up. You can search and expand the full directory, which should make people easier to find as it grows.

Thanks to 小池, and to everyone adding information, translating, coding, testing, and sending suggestions. A lot of the little fixes have come out of that help.

LABs, Memory Corridor, and the AI Observation Terminal are still being adjusted. Features may change, pause, or be removed, and updates may reset progress, conversations, and related data. Keep another copy of anything important.

This is an unofficial fan project. AI-generated content does not represent the artists or official positions. When researching something, remember to check the reliable sources cited in the articles.

Keep letting me know when something breaks. And if you want to help fill in the Wiki, come on in.

See you at the next observation.
